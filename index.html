Cybersecurity Basics

Protecting Your Digital Footprint

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of defending computers, servers, mobile devices, electronic systems, networks, and data from malicious digital attacks. It is also known as information technology security or electronic information security.

The Three Pillars (CIA Triad)

A robust security strategy centers around three primary principles:

  • Confidentiality: Ensuring that sensitive data is accessed only by authorized individuals.
  • Integrity: Guarding information against improper modification or destruction to guarantee its accuracy.
  • Availability: Making sure that authorized users have reliable and timely access to their data and systems.

Essential Security Best Practices

Maintaining basic digital hygiene significantly minimizes the risk of successful cyber threats:

  • Use strong, unique passwords for every online account.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) wherever possible.
  • Keep operating systems, browsers, and applications updated to patch security vulnerabilities.
  • Verify links and email senders carefully to avoid falling victim to phishing traps.
