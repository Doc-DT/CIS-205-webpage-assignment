What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, devices, and personal information from digital attacks, theft, and unauthorized access.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

  • Phishing: Fake emails or messages designed to steal personal information.
  • Malware: Harmful software that can damage devices or steal data.
  • Password Attacks: Attempts to guess or steal your passwords.
  • Identity Theft: Using someone's personal information without permission.

How to Stay Safe Online

  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.
  • Keep your software and devices updated.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi networks.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Never share sensitive information with untrusted sources.
